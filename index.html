About SHREENATHJI VASTRIVA

Considered fashion, for how you actually dress.

SHREENATHJI VASTRIVA is a fashion brand built around one idea: clothing should feel intentional. We design and curate for both women and men, bringing together modern silhouettes and traditional Indian craftsmanship in a single wardrobe.

Our women's collection spans sarees, suits, kurtis, lehengas and ethnic sets alongside western wear — dresses, tops, jeans and trousers — so festive dressing and everyday dressing both get the same attention to fit and fabric. Our men's collection covers kurtas, sherwanis and ethnic sets through to shirts, jeans, blazers and formalwear, built for versatility across occasions.

We keep our focus on fabric quality — sourcing and finishing pieces that hold up to daily wear, not just a single occasion — and on a customer-first approach to sizing, ordering and support, whether you're shopping on the website or reaching out to us directly on WhatsApp.
